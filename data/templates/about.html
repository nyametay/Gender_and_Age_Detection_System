About Our Gender & Age Detection System

Our Gender & Age Detection System leverages advanced computer vision and deep learning models to accurately estimate a person's gender and age from an image. Whether you're experimenting with AI or integrating this into a real-world application, our system ensures speed, accuracy, and user-friendly interaction.

The system uses two powerful models to deliver predictions, giving you the ability to compare results for greater reliability. Combined with an intuitive interface, this tool is designed for both casual exploration and professional use.

Key Features

  • Dual-model prediction
  • High accuracy and speed
  • Dark mode support
  • Fully responsive UI

Technology Stack

  • Flask (Python Backend)
  • OpenCV & Deep Learning
  • TailwindCSS for styling
  • SQLite for storage
